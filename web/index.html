How to play

Touch

  • Left thumb: move. Push further to run.
  • Right thumb: drag anywhere to turn (dragging from Fire turns while you shoot).
  • Use opens doors and presses switches. ‹ › change weapon, Map shows the map, ☰ opens the menu (save, load, quit).
  • In menus: the arrows, OK and Back. Turn the phone sideways.

Keyboard and mouse

Click the game to capture the mouse. WASD move, mouse turns, click or Ctrl fires, Space uses, Esc menu, Tab map, 1-7 weapons.

Gamepad

Pair it with the device first; press a button once the game is running.

Saves

Saved games stay in this browser on this device.
